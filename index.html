How to play

On your turn, place 1 Viking on an empty resource space next to any Viking or a longship in the ocean. Take that resource and put it anywhere on your fjord.

  • Stacks: each House starts with 2 tiles, each Watchtower with 2 and each Castle with 3. The number on a building shows how many are left; an empty building gives nothing.
  • House: your new Viking is next to it. Take 1 house.
  • Watchtower: link two towers with a chain of your Vikings. Take 1 tile for each tower. Linking a new tower to towers you already linked gives 2 more: 1 for the new tower and 1 for the tower it joins.
  • Castle: a chain of 4 of your Vikings touching a castle gives 1 tile. A chain of 8 gives a 2nd tile and a chain of 12 gives a 3rd.
  • Longships: at the end of your turn you may take 1 from the ocean and put it on your fjord. Put its 3 resources around it to flip it for a bonus. Each unfilled longship costs 5 points at the end.
  • Construction sites: surround them with what they show to score 5, 7 or 9.
  • Shields (once each): place a second Viking, stack on an occupied space, or take 2 of the resource.
  • Trophies: once per game, claim one needing no more axes than you have.
  • Scoring: for castles (4), watchtowers (2), houses (1), gold (2), sheep (1) and wood (1), add your longship bonuses to the starting value and multiply by how many you have.
